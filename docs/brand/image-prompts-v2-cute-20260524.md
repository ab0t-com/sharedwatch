# sharedwatch — image generation prompts (v2: cute cartoon, J×A corporate-launch register)

A complete alternate brand direction for the same fifteen images defined in [`image-prompts-20260524.md`](image-prompts-20260524.md) (the "v1, photographic, watchmaker's-bench" register). Same uses. Same aspect ratios — so the shape-checker pipeline is identical. Same product story.

What changes: **the visual language**. v1 is leather-bound photography. v2 is the friendly, restrained illustration register you see on a Japanese tech-company product page (think Nintendo, Pokémon Center, Muji's app illustrations) blended with American workplace-software warmth (Notion, Linear, Slack circa their best mascot era, Stripe Press' friendlier illustrations, Apple App Store editorial cards). Cute without being childish. Soft without being twee. Professional without being cold.

Pick v1 if the audience is "developers who notice the weight of a well-balanced wrench". Pick v2 if the audience is "everyone else, plus those same developers on their day off". Run both, compare in context, decide later. The two systems are designed to be mutually exclusive — don't mix.

---

## Brand foundation (v2 — read once before any image)

**The product story is unchanged.** Calm, durable, pull-based activity journal for a local shared folder. Cooperative multi-actor coordination. Single binary. Local. Honest. Everything in v1's "what this is" applies here — only the *feeling* differs.

**The feeling we're chasing in v2.**
Warm but restrained. Cute but credible. The aesthetic of a thoughtful Japanese tech announcement: a clean stage, soft pastel sky, a friendly mascot demonstrating one feature at a time, no clutter, no panic. The energy of opening a beautifully-designed Nintendo Switch box. The same emotional contract as v1 — "we take care of your stuff" — delivered through softer body language.

**The feelings we're avoiding.**
Saccharine kids-TV. Aggressive corporate-cheer (Slack 2018 hi-five emojis). Generic Lottie-loader pastel. Cyber-cute (anime-glitch hybrids). Western-comic snark. Anything that says "growth hack" or "startup launch announcement on Product Hunt".

### Visual system (v2)

**Palette.** Pick from this set; use no more than four hues in any one image, plus the single warm accent.

| Role | Hex | When |
|---|---|---|
| Peach cream | `#FCEFE6` | Primary backgrounds, soft skies, paper surfaces |
| Mint sage | `#C7E5D4` | Secondary surface, gentle foliage, calm water |
| Misty lavender | `#D4D0E8` | Atmospheric secondary, far backgrounds, shadow on cream |
| Cloud white | `#FAFAF7` | Highlights, breathing space, page surface |
| Deep slate | `#3D4A66` | Line work, eyes, deepest shadow — **never** pure black |
| **Warm accent: soft yellow** | `#F5C84B` | Lantern glows, sun, single happy spotlight |
| **Warm accent: coral** | `#F08F7E` | Hearts, blushes, friendly indicator dots |

**Critical rule.** Use **exactly one** warm accent per image (yellow *or* coral, not both). The accent appears once or twice — never spread across the scene.

**Lines.** Hand-drawn quality — slight wobble, varied weight, never perfectly geometric. Use deep slate `#3D4A66`. Line weight ~2–3px at 1080px output, slightly heavier at the silhouette edge, lighter on internal details.

**Shapes.** Rounded everything. No sharp angles. Imagine every shape was first traced in pencil by a tired-but-careful illustrator at the end of a good day. Pebble-soft. Slight overlap allowed; perfect tangents not required.

**Faces (when present).** Two dots for eyes, simple curved smile, optional single coral blush oval on each cheek. **No mouth open in surprise**, no toothy grins, no anxiety lines. Default expression: calm contentment. Eyes closed in a soft U-curve is also fine ("attentive listening" energy).

**Mascots (when scene allows).** Objects can be gently personified — a notebook with a calm face, a clock with sleepy eyes, a lighthouse with a friendly lantern-glow that reads as "an eye". **Do not** introduce humanoid robots, glowing AI brains, or any creature that looks like it's selling crypto. When in doubt, leave the object un-faced.

**Texture.** Optional subtle paper grain overlay (very gentle, ~5% opacity). No gradients except: (a) a soft sky-to-horizon fade in outdoor scenes, (b) a soft glow around the warm accent. Otherwise flat.

**Composition.** Centred or rule-of-thirds. Generous negative space — these are **single-feature** illustrations, not busy product diagrams. Subject should survive cropping to 1:1.

**Strict don'ts (v2-specific).**
- No screens visible anywhere.
- No on-image text (except prompt #15, which is explicitly the wordmark card).
- No anthropomorphic humanoid robots.
- No "blast lines" / radial impact graphics (Product-Hunt-launch energy).
- No 3D rendering (no soft 3D cute models — think 2D illustration only).
- No isometric perspective (too tech-bro).
- No glittery sparkle effects.
- No clip-art arrows or callout bubbles.

**Style references to share with the artist.**
Notion's marketing illustrations (people working at desks). Linear's app illustrations. Studio Ghibli's quieter background scenes (the bus-stop scene in *Totoro*; the early morning in *Whisper of the Heart*). Sanrio's understated characters (Pompompurin, Cinnamoroll's *backgrounds*, not the characters themselves). Apple App Store editorial illustrations. Mailchimp's older illustration era. Stripe Press' friendlier covers. The watercolour-feeling vector work on Headspace.

## 1. HERO — *Friendly lighthouse at dawn over a calm pastel sea*

**Use:** Landing-page hero. The single most important image in this set.
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 landscape.
**Mood in one sentence:** A small friend who's been awake all night, happy that morning is here.

**Background layer.** A wide pastel-peach sky filling the upper two-thirds, fading down toward soft mint at the horizon. A single soft-yellow round sun, low and warm, placed in the upper-left third — flat, no rays, just a calm warm circle with a barely-perceptible halo.

**Middle layer.** A calm mint-sage sea taking the bottom third, drawn as gentle wavy horizontal bands of slightly varying mint shades. One or two soft curve lines hint at slow swells, no breakers. Far in the distance, a tiny rounded sail-boat the size of a thumbnail, in misty lavender — barely there, but it gives scale.

**Subject layer.** A short, slightly chubby lighthouse standing on a small rounded rocky base in the right-third of the frame. Cylindrical body in cloud-white with three or four wrap-around peach-cream stripes. A small dome roof on top in deep slate. The lantern room glows with a soft yellow circle — *this is the warm accent*. Two tiny calm dot-eyes are implied on the lantern (or perhaps clearly drawn as a friendly soft face — artist's call, but tilt friendly). Slight coral blush on the lighthouse's cheeks (where the lantern stripes meet).

**Foreground layer.** A few rounded pebble shapes at the lighthouse's base, in deeper mint. One small seabird flying low across the sky (a simple soft "M" curve, no detail). Optional: two tiny soft-blob clouds in the upper right of the sky.

**Style notes.** Everything rounded. The lighthouse is *friendly*, not heroic. The sun is a calm circle, not a sunburst. The whole composition should feel like the title card of a gentle morning anime — "today will be okay".

**Palette.** Peach cream sky, mint sea, cloud-white lighthouse, deep slate line-work, **single soft yellow accent** at the lantern (and only there).

**Style references.** Studio Ghibli's *From Up on Poppy Hill* harbour scenes. The opening shots of Pokémon HOME promotional art.

**Avoid.** Sharp rocks. Storm clouds. A beam of light slicing through fog. Lighthouse with a stern face. A bright orange sun (use soft yellow only). A second lighthouse.

**Why this works.** The lighthouse-as-friend reads instantly. The pastel palette signals "calm" before you read any text. There is generous space upper-left for a wordmark and tagline overlay.

## 2. REPO BANNER — *Calm flat-lay of cartoon journal, compass, and tools*

**Use:** GitHub README banner, social cover, blog series header.
**Aspect ratio:** 3:1 ultra-wide.
**Mood in one sentence:** A tidy desk full of warm little friends, ready to start the day.

**Background layer.** A flat warm peach-cream surface filling the frame (the "desk"). Subtle paper-grain texture at 5% opacity. A single soft mint shadow band runs horizontally across the lower fifth to suggest a desk edge.

**Middle layer.** Items laid out evenly across the width with breathing room:
- **(far left)** An open notebook with calm cartoon eyes on the cover corner. Two pages visible, filled with gentle squiggle-lines suggesting handwriting (don't draw real letters). A tiny dotted-line arrow in the margin (the "ref" link metaphor).
- **(centre-left)** A round brass compass with a single tiny coral arrow needle pointing northeast. The numerals on the dial are tiny soft dots, not real numbers.
- **(centre)** A folded paper map, partially open, showing three tiny mint-coloured circles ("watch points") connected by a dotted slate path.
- **(centre-right)** A magnifying glass with a soft yellow circle inside the lens (the warm accent — implies "sun shines through").
- **(far right)** A simple chunky fountain pen with a soft slate body, resting on a folded cloud-white cloth.

**Subject layer.** No single hero — the *arrangement reads left-to-right* as a sentence: note, orient, map, examine, write.

**Foreground layer.** A pressed leaf (mint, soft) in the notebook gutter. A few subtle pencil dots scattered near the pen. Optional: one or two tiny coral hearts near the notebook to suggest the workspace is loved (use sparingly).

**Style notes.** Flat, slightly textured, all objects in the same illustration style. The whole arrangement should feel like a desk a small friendly creature would set up the night before to be ready in the morning.

**Palette.** Peach cream desk, mint sage and misty lavender objects, cloud white where things rest, deep slate outlines, **soft yellow accent** in the magnifying-glass lens only.

**Style references.** Notion's "team meeting" illustration series. Muji app onboarding screens.

**Avoid.** Modern objects (laptop, headphones, branded cup). Photographic textures. Anything sharp-edged. More than one or two coral elements.

**Why this works.** Same story as v1's banner (multiple hands, careful observation, durable tools), told in the v2 register. Wide format leaves room to overlay a wordmark + tagline on the right.

## 3. SQUARE ICON — *A round friendly "watch creature" face*

**Use:** App icon (favicon, PWA, system tray), GitHub social card, profile picture.
**Aspect ratio:** 1:1 square.
**Mood in one sentence:** A little friend whose whole job is to gently notice things.

**Background layer.** Flat peach-cream square fill. Subtle paper grain at 5% opacity.

**Middle layer.** Nothing — the creature sits directly on the background.

**Subject layer.** A perfectly centred round body that is also a stylised lighthouse seen head-on: a soft cloud-white circle with two horizontal peach-cream stripes wrapping around it. At the top, a small soft-yellow dome (the "lantern roof"). Inside the body, two large calm dot-eyes (closed in a soft U-curve OR open as plain round dots — the artist picks one and the whole brand commits). Two tiny coral blush ovals on the cheeks. No mouth (calm friend, not chatty).

**Foreground layer.** Nothing — single-subject icon.

**Style notes.** Must read clearly at 16×16 (favicon) and at 1024×1024 (app store). Test at both sizes. The face should be *the* readable element. The lighthouse stripes are a bonus once you're close enough.

**Palette.** Peach cream background, cloud white body, peach stripes, deep slate eyes/outline, **soft yellow accent** on the dome roof. **No coral if blush is omitted; coral *only* on cheeks if blush is included.**

**Style references.** Cinnamoroll-style Sanrio minimalism. Pompompurin's understated face. Pokémon Snorlax's gentle expression.

**Avoid.** Anything sharp or geometric. A mouth (especially toothy). A robotic or futuristic feel. Multiple colour accents. Lens-flare on the dome.

**Why this works.** This is the mascot the rest of v2 can lean on. It's a one-glance friend — favicon, profile pic, social-share thumbnail. The lighthouse-face fuses the brand symbol with the brand mascot, so we don't need two separate marks.

## 4. CALM — *A bonsai with sleeping eyes*

**Use:** Section illustration for "calm" / "no interruptions", blog inline.
**Aspect ratio:** 3:2 editorial landscape.
**Mood in one sentence:** Patience as a tiny round friend.

**Background layer.** Flat peach-cream wall. Subtle horizontal misty-lavender shadow band low across the bottom to suggest "wall meets table". Soft paper grain.

**Middle layer.** A simple low table drawn as a rounded mint-sage horizontal stripe with two short soft legs.

**Subject layer.** A small bonsai tree, centred on the table. Trunk drawn as one or two rounded curves in deep slate. Foliage rendered as 4–6 soft mint-and-sage cloud-puff clusters of different sizes, arranged in the classical asymmetric bonsai shape. Optional: two tiny closed-eye U-curves on one foliage cluster (the bonsai is "napping"). The pot is a soft slate stoneware shape with one peach-cream horizontal stripe.

**Foreground layer.** Two or three tiny round pebbles in front of the pot. Optional: one tiny fallen mint leaf next to a pebble. A soft yellow circle in the upper-right corner (the "skylight" — the warm accent).

**Style notes.** The bonsai is the friend; the pot is its bed; the skylight is the gentle morning. Whole image should make a tired viewer relax their shoulders.

**Palette.** Peach cream wall, mint sage foliage and table, misty lavender shadow, deep slate trunk and outlines, **soft yellow accent** in the corner skylight only.

**Style references.** Studio Ghibli's gentle interior scenes (the early-morning kitchen in *Whisper of the Heart*). Notion's "focus mode" illustrations.

**Avoid.** A complex tree. Drama. Multiple pots. A gardener figure. Photo-real leaves.

**Why this works.** Visualises "passive cadence" as *a friend taking a long calm breath*. The skylight as warm accent ties it back to the brand without shouting.

## 5. DURABILITY — *A stack of friendly cartoon books with calm eyes*

**Use:** Section illustration for "durable journal" / "restart-safe", changelog page header.
**Aspect ratio:** 3:2 editorial landscape (also works as 1:1).
**Mood in one sentence:** A whole stack of trustworthy little friends, each holding years of careful records.

**Background layer.** Flat peach-cream wall. Subtle paper grain. A soft misty-lavender horizontal band low across the bottom for the shelf surface.

**Middle layer.** Nothing — the books sit directly on the shelf.

**Subject layer.** A stack of 5–6 chunky rounded books, alternating in palette colours: one mint sage, one misty lavender, one cloud white, one peach cream, one deep slate (with a soft-yellow band as decoration). Each spine has a calm pair of dot-eyes (closed U-curves preferred — these books are *resting calmly*, holding their content safely). One small coral bookmark ribbon hangs out of the second-from-top book. The top book is open across the stack, showing two pages with cartoon squiggle-handwriting filling them.

**Foreground layer.** One small fountain pen resting on the open book's pages, slightly diagonal, with a deep-slate body and a single tiny soft-yellow dot for the nib.

**Style notes.** Books are characters. Their faces are sleepy-content, never alarmed. Stack should be slightly off-axis (lived-in), not military-perfect.

**Palette.** Peach cream wall, mixed cool pastels for book spines, deep slate outlines, cloud-white open pages, **single coral accent** on the bookmark ribbon (this is one of the few scenes that uses coral, not yellow).

**Style references.** The bookshelves in Studio Ghibli's *Kiki's Delivery Service*. Notion's docs-section illustrations.

**Avoid.** Scrolls, wax seals, fantasy decorations. Books with worried/scared faces. Stacked too high (it would look toppling — that's the wrong energy).

**Why this works.** "Your data lasts" — visualised as a row of small friends holding it safely. The closed-eye faces signal *trust*, not alertness.

## 6. MULTI-ACTOR COORDINATION — *Three friendly creatures at three workbenches sharing a central table*

**Use:** Section illustration for multi-root / multi-actor coordination, collaboration blog header.
**Aspect ratio:** 3:2 editorial landscape.
**Mood in one sentence:** Three small friends doing their own work, happy to share the toolbox in the middle.

**Background layer.** Soft peach-cream interior wall, suggestion of a single arched window in the upper-centre (a simple rounded rectangle with soft yellow inside — the warm accent and the room's only light source). Light paper grain.

**Middle layer.** Three rounded mint-sage workbenches arranged at angles to each other, as the three points of a soft triangle. Each bench has the in-progress work of one craft, rendered minimally:
- Left bench: a few coils of soft thread (leather-stitching, abstracted).
- Centre bench: a small open notebook + a tiny ink-pot.
- Right bench: a couple of round clock-gear shapes laid out.

**Subject layer.** A long narrow misty-lavender central table between them, holding the *shared* tools: a tape-measure circle, a magnifying-glass shape, a single hammer (simple), a small kettle on a stove. At each workbench, a *very simple* creature character with no fixed species — round body, two dot eyes, simple smile, slight blush. Each in a slightly different soft pastel (one mint, one peach, one lavender — matching their bench atmosphere). All three creatures face inward toward the shared central table.

**Foreground layer.** A few pebbles or floor-detail at the bottom edge. Optional: a single soft-yellow halo around the window glow.

**Style notes.** No creature is bigger or more central than the others. They're peers. The shared table is the *load-bearing object* — visually the centre of gravity.

**Palette.** Peach cream walls, mint sage and misty lavender benches and creatures, cloud white shared tools, deep slate outlines, **single soft yellow accent** in the window.

**Style references.** Notion's "collaboration" illustration series. Studio Ghibli's craft-workshop scenes (the witch's broom-shop in *Kiki's Delivery Service*).

**Avoid.** A boss/manager character. Three identical creatures (boring). A leader at a central position. Any character looking grumpy or competitive.

**Why this works.** The shared central table is the journal; the three creatures are the actors; their independent work plus shared infrastructure is exactly the product's coordination model. The friendly peer geometry says "no hierarchy".

## 7. PULL VS PUSH — *A calm cartoon tide gauge on a harbour wall, with an inviting empty bench*

**Use:** Section illustration for "pull-based, not push", blog explainer on cadence.
**Aspect ratio:** 4:3 (portrait-ish landscape).
**Mood in one sentence:** A gentle friend on the wall who waits patiently for you to come look.

**Background layer.** Peach-cream sky in the upper third, fading to soft mint at the horizon. A few misty-lavender soft cloud-blobs floating in the sky.

**Middle layer.** Calm mint-sage water in the lower fifth, drawn as two soft horizontal wavy bands. Far in the distance, a tiny sail-boat (the same size and style as in image #1, for brand consistency).

**Subject layer.** The harbour wall — a chunky soft rectangle in misty lavender taking the middle of the frame — with a round mechanical tide-gauge mounted on it. The gauge is a circle with a peach-cream face, simple dot-and-line markings around the rim, and a single thin slate needle pointing to a relaxed mid-position. The whole gauge has two tiny dot-eyes (closed in calm U-curves) and a small soft smile — it's *content*, just doing its job.

**Foreground layer.** Below the gauge, a small mint-sage bench, empty, awaiting a visitor. Optional: a few small pebbles around the bench's legs. **One small soft-yellow circle** above the gauge — a gentle warm spotlight (the warm accent, just enough to make the gauge feel "noticed").

**Style notes.** Whole image conveys *available, not demanding*. The empty bench is an open invitation.

**Palette.** Peach cream sky, mint sea and bench, misty lavender wall and clouds, cloud-white gauge face, deep slate outlines and needle, **single soft yellow accent** spotlight above the gauge.

**Style references.** The harbour-side scenes in Studio Ghibli's *Ponyo*. Pokémon HOME's harbour-area background art.

**Avoid.** A user at the bench (the bench is for the *viewer*). A digital display. A storm. Anyone running for the gauge.

**Why this works.** A pull-based metaphor made cute: information sits patiently and friendlily, available whenever you walk over. The empty bench is the most important emotional element — it's *your* seat.

## 8. AMBIENT AWARENESS — *A friendly flap-board in a quiet station, one calm character on a bench*

**Use:** Section illustration for "ambient awareness, no interruption", landing-page sub-hero.
**Aspect ratio:** 16:9 wide landscape.
**Mood in one sentence:** Updates happening calmly in the background; you'll glance over when you're ready.

**Background layer.** A simple cartoon station interior: a peach-cream wall with three arched windows running across the upper third, each filled with a soft sunset-pastel fade (peach into soft yellow). A single ceiling lamp (round, soft yellow inside — the warm accent) hangs in the upper-right.

**Middle layer.** A cloud-white tiled floor across the lower third, drawn flat (no perspective). On the right wall, a large rectangular flap-board indicator with rounded corners. The board shows rows of soft squiggle "text" suggesting departure information. One row is mid-flip (a few squiggle pieces are angled and slightly blurred to imply gentle motion). The board has two tiny dot-eyes and a calm smile — it's relaxed, enjoying its work.

**Subject layer.** A single small creature character sitting on a long curved mint-sage bench, in the lower-left third. Round body in misty lavender, calm closed-eye U-curves, no mouth. They're holding a small folded cloud-white shape (a "newspaper"). Their attention is on the paper, not the board.

**Foreground layer.** The end of the bench at the lower-right. A small misty-lavender bag set on the floor beside it.

**Style notes.** Everything calm. The board's gentle motion is the only "activity" — and even that is soft. The character is *content*, not avoiding the board; they're aware it's there and they don't need to look right now.

**Palette.** Peach cream walls, soft yellow window glow and lamp, mint bench, misty lavender character and bag, cloud-white floor and "paper", deep slate outlines, **soft yellow accent** in the windows and ceiling lamp (consistent warm source).

**Style references.** Studio Ghibli's bus-stop scene from *My Neighbor Totoro*. Notion's "focus" page illustration.

**Avoid.** A rushed traveller. A digital screen. A board with shocking news (alarm-red). Anyone running.

**Why this works.** Captures the literal product promise: information updates calmly whether you're watching or not, and your job is to be *present in your own work*, glancing over only when you choose. The character is the user, modelled as a friend.

## 10. THE JOURNAL — *An open cartoon notebook with multiple cute handwritings*

**Use:** Documentation header, "what gets stored" explainer, blog post header on data shape.
**Aspect ratio:** 3:2 editorial landscape.
**Mood in one sentence:** One friendly notebook, many gentle hands writing in it, every page kept.

**Background layer.** Flat peach-cream desk surface. Subtle paper grain.

**Middle layer.** Nothing — the notebook dominates.

**Subject layer.** A large cloud-white open notebook, drawn flat (no perspective), taking 80% of the frame. Two facing pages, each filled with several "entries". Each entry uses *visually distinct squiggle styles* — one row is small loopy squiggles (handwriting A), another is angular zigzag squiggles (handwriting B), another is dotted dashes (handwriting C). Each entry is preceded by a small bullet — a circle for some, a square for others, a small heart for one — to suggest different "actors". A few entries have tiny illustrations in the margins: a mint leaf, a single coral heart, a tiny soft-yellow sun.

**Foreground layer.** A small pressed leaf in the gutter (mint). A small chunky pencil resting at the bottom-right of the open spread, deep slate body with a soft-yellow tip (warm accent). A misty-lavender paperweight pinning the upper-left corner.

**Style notes.** Notebook itself can have a tiny calm face on the upper corner of the open page — entirely optional and a delicate choice. The *handwriting variation* is the most important narrative element: it must read as "multiple writers, one document".

**Palette.** Peach cream desk, cloud-white pages, deep slate squiggle handwriting in three subtly different shades, mint leaf, misty lavender paperweight, **single soft yellow accent** on the pencil tip (and one tiny optional sun-doodle in the margin).

**Style references.** Notion's docs-page editorial illustrations. The journal pages in Studio Ghibli's *Whisper of the Heart*.

**Avoid.** Real letters or words (squiggles only — too easy to accidentally write something off-brand). All entries in the same handwriting style. A diary lock (wrong vibe).

**Why this works.** Visualises `events.payload_json` with `actor`: multiple writers, one durable shared document, every entry attributed by its visual style, references possible between entries (the margin doodles). The cute register makes "shared journal" feel inviting, not surveillance-y.

## Notes for whoever runs these prompts

- **Run each prompt at its declared aspect ratio.** The reshape-checker is the same checker as v1 uses; the artistic intent assumes the declared shape.
- **Generate 4–8 variants per prompt** and pick the one that feels most aligned with the brand foundation, not the one with the most detail. *Less is more* applies even harder in v2.
- **Resist drift toward saccharine.** If a generated image starts pushing toward sticker-pack cuteness, glitter, or kawaii-overload, regenerate. The target is *Studio Ghibli's gentlest moments*, not *Lisa Frank trapper-keeper*.
- **Hold the line on the palette.** One warm accent per image. If you find yourself using yellow *and* coral in the same scene, pull one out.
- **The mascot from #3 appears in #1, #14, and #15.** Keep its proportions consistent across those four images so it reads as the same creature.
- **No on-image text in any prompt except #15.** Wordmarks and taglines belong in the CSS layer above the image, not baked in.
- **Save selections under** `docs/brand/assets-v2/<prompt-number>-<short-name>.png`. Keep originals at full resolution; produce web derivatives separately. Mirror the v1 asset path scheme so a future renderer can A/B between sets.
- **Re-read the v2 "strict don'ts" before approving any image.** That list is the brand boundary; everything inside is fine.
